The first public release of StudioVD: a desktop tool to put CAD parts together, make them
move like the real mechanism, and map what they do in a schematic that solves as you edit.

## What it does

- **Parts**: import STEP, IGES, glTF/GLB, OBJ and STL, or a downloaded `.zip` of them
  (native CAD files inside are listed with the advice to export STEP). Materials with real
  looks and densities, glass and transparent plastics, X-ray and wireframe views.
- **Assembling**: anchors snapped to faces, edges and holes; fixed, revolute, prismatic and
  spherical mates with limits; gears, rack and pinion and lead screws coupled; degrees of
  freedom and conflicts shown in the tree.
- **Test mode**: drag a part and everything mated to it follows its joints, with collisions.
- **Library**: generated standard parts (fasteners, bearings, gears, pulleys and belts,
  springs, profiles, enclosures, pots, knobs, jacks, footswitches, LEDs, batteries, boards,
  cable clips) and an online catalog of signed packs (the first: electronic components and
  boards from KiCad's libraries). Save your own components and share them as packs.
- **Cables**: wires of the length you cut, through clips and waypoints, laid as arcs, waves or
  a coil, one wire or a twisted pair; too-short warnings and a cut list.
- **Schematic and simulation**: electrical, mechanical, fluid, light and data blocks, solved
  in steady state as you edit; bindings carry hinge angles, weights, contacts and light
  into the solver, and glows, tints, arrows and labels back onto the parts.
- **Examples** (Help › Examples), including a see-through guitar pedal whose Volume knob
  dims its LED in test mode.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256.

The installers are **not code-signed**. On Windows, SmartScreen warns the first time ("More
info" › "Run anyway"); the Linux files carry no signature, so check them against the sums.
Installing over this version later keeps your projects and library.

## Good to know

- StudioVD works offline. It goes online only when you ask (the catalog's Refresh and
  Download, the connection test) or if you turn on the weekly catalog check, and asks once
  before the first connection. Edit › Network Settings has offline mode and proxy settings.
- Simulation is steady state only. macOS is not supported.
- Projects are `.stvd` files that hold their geometry, textures and licences, so they open
  without the library.
- StudioVD itself is not open-source licensed (all rights reserved). Third-party licences
  are in Help › About › Third-party notices; catalog content keeps its own licence (KiCad's
  libraries: CC-BY-SA-4.0 with the KiCad libraries exception), listed in Help › Project
  Credits for every project that uses it.
