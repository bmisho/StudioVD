Fixes and additions from the first round of testing 0.2.0 on Windows. The guitar pedal is
under Help › Examples › Guitar pedal.

## Changes

- **Move parts by dragging them.** In the 3D view, drag a part, or a component you dropped
  from the Library, to slide it along the ground; hold **Shift** to move it straight up or
  down. Everything mated to it moves along, and each drag is one undo step. The gizmo (T, R)
  is still there for exact moves.
- **One menu on right click.** Windows also showed the webview's own menu (Back, Refresh,
  Print) over StudioVD's. It no longer does, and F5, Ctrl+R, Alt+Left and Ctrl+P no longer
  reload or leave the app (a reload would have lost unsaved work).
- **Box selection.** Drag with the left button over empty space: in the 3D view, rightward
  selects what lies entirely inside the box, leftward what it touches (Ctrl adds to the
  selection); in the schematic, the box selects every block it touches. The schematic now
  pans with the middle or right button, like the 3D view.
- **Place Anchor places one anchor, then goes back to Select.** Before, it stayed on, so a
  later click on a part placed another anchor. Hold **Shift** to place several in a row.
  Selecting a part or component no longer draws its anchors either (they looked like new
  ones); View › Show Anchors of Selected Parts brings that back.
- **Position anchors exactly.** An anchor's Properties add *Place again on the part…* (click
  where it goes; it keeps its mates and cables), *Point along* ±X/±Y/±Z, *Turn 90°* and
  *In*/*Out* along its direction by a step you set, next to the origin fields.
- **Route cables by clicking.** A cable's Route has *Add waypoints by clicking*: click
  surfaces in the 3D view in the order the cable should pass them (Esc to finish). Each
  waypoint can be placed again (crosshair) or moved up and down the route.
- **Euroboxes.** The Library has Euro stacking containers in 21 sizes: 200 × 150, 300 × 200,
  400 × 300, 600 × 400 and 800 × 600 footprints in the heights they are sold in (any height
  from 60 to 600 mm in Properties). Their rim and stacking seat mate, so one stacks on another.
- **The online catalog is easier to find.** Its bar (status, Refresh, Packs) now shows under
  All sources too: press Refresh, then download a pack to get the electronic parts and
  boards from KiCad's libraries.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256. The installers are not code-signed: on Windows,
SmartScreen warns the first time ("More info" › "Run anyway"). Installing over 0.2.0 keeps
your projects and library.
