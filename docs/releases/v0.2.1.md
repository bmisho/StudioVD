Fixes from the first round of testing 0.2.0 on Windows.

## Changes

- **Move parts by dragging them.** In the 3D view, drag a part, or a component you dropped
  from the Library, to slide it along the ground; hold **Shift** to move it straight up or
  down. Everything mated to it moves along, and each drag is one undo step. The gizmo (T, R)
  is still there for exact moves.
- **One menu on right click.** Windows also showed the webview's own menu (Back, Refresh,
  Print) over StudioVD's. It no longer does, and F5, Ctrl+R, Alt+Left and Ctrl+P no longer
  reload or leave the app (a reload would have lost unsaved work).
- **The online catalog is easier to find.** Its bar (status, Refresh, Packs) now shows under
  All sources too: press Refresh, then download a pack to get the electronic parts and
  boards from KiCad's libraries.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256. The installers are not code-signed: on Windows,
SmartScreen warns the first time ("More info" › "Run anyway"). Installing over 0.2.0 keeps
your projects and library.
