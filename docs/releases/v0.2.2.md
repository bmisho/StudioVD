The second round of fixes and additions from testing 0.2.0 on Windows. It includes everything
in 0.2.1 (one right-click menu, dragging parts to move them, box selection, anchor
positioning, click-routed cables, Euroboxes), whose installers were never published: its
release build stopped on two test-runner faults.

## Changes

- **Cable tool** (toolbar): click where a cable starts (a lug, a pad, any surface), click
  zip ties on the way, then where it ends. The cable is cut to its route, and the tool stays
  on for the next one (Esc to stop).
- **Zip ties that fit.** Zip ties are now flat straps with a head and a cut tail, and there is
  a **Zip tie on a mount** (adhesive base) for walls. A tie sizes itself to the cables the
  Cable tool runs through it; its Properties show the bundle and offer **Fit to its cables**.
- **Cables no longer pass through parts.** In the 3D view a cable is pushed out of any part it
  would cross and lies on its surface instead (test mode still draws cables as laid).
- **Move or turn a whole assembly.** **Edit › Select All** (Ctrl+A), a box selection, or an
  assembly's group gets one move/rotate gizmo (T, R); everything mated comes along, one undo
  step. A single dropped component is still moved by dragging it.
- **The guitar pedal** (Help › Examples): Volume, Gain and Tone are all in the schematic and each
  knob changes the LED; the footswitch's plunger can be stamped on in test mode and latches
  the LED off and on, like a real stomp switch; zip ties on mounts hold the cables; no more
  wavy or coiled cables.
- **Schematic wires stay solid** while current flows (moving beads on the wire, not gaps that
  looked like a broken or dotted wire), and the pedal's schematic keeps every net on its own
  lines.
- **Results** list each block's power as **Supplies** or **Uses** (no negative numbers).
